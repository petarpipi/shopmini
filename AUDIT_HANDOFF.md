## Product and scope

ShopMini is a frontend-only premium e-commerce portfolio demonstration for the fictional AURA skincare brand. The interface and documentation are English. User-facing collaboration is Macedonian. No deployment or remote push is authorized.

The key story is: local catalog → variant/quantity → bag → customer information → shipping information → review → truthful local confirmation. Wishlist and bag persist; personal form information does not.

## Review targets

1. `src/lib/commerce.ts`: integer cents, shipping boundary, size-aware merges, quantity bounds, filtering/sorting and storage sanitization.
2. `src/state/Store.tsx`: valid data restoration, no mount-time overwrite, persistent updates, cross-tab synchronization, unavailable storage, and toast lifecycle.
3. `src/pages/Checkout.tsx`: step validation and editing, accurate snapshot totals, duplicate-click prevention, no card fields, no network submission and cleanup of personal data after confirmation.
4. `src/pages/Shop.tsx`: combined URL parameters, result count, reset and empty state.
5. `src/components/Shell.tsx`: native modal focus, Escape, route changes, mobile navigation, search, newsletter and skip link.
6. `src/pages/Product.tsx`: selected size, quantities, stock, images, disclosures and related items.
7. `src/pages/Contact.tsx`: validation, first-error focus and explicit unsent success message.
8. `e2e/store.spec.ts`: actual shopping journeys, refresh persistence, empty/unavailable cases, responsive widths, broken images, runtime errors and axe checks.
9. `ASSET_CREDITS.md` and `public/licenses/`: actual asset provenance and notices.

## Invariants

- Only `shopmini-aura-v1` may be written in LocalStorage by the app.
- Customer information must not be stored or transmitted.
- A bag line is a known in-stock product with a valid size and integer quantity 1–10.
- Checkout totals must use the same price/shipping rules as the bag.
- Malformed entries must not discard valid sibling cart/wishlist records.
- No real payment, submitted order, delivered contact message, or newsletter subscription may be claimed.
- No invented testimonials, certifications, clinical benefits or sales numbers.
- No remote deployment or push. Preserve independent Git isolation.
